# 🌌 Quantum Gravity Sandbox

**Quantum Gravity Sandbox** is an interactive, real-time particle simulation built entirely with standard HTML5 Canvas and Vanilla JavaScript. Users can place gravitational attractors and repulsors to manipulate thousands of dynamic particles, simulating orbital trajectories, black holes, and supernova expansions in a browser environment.

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Zero Dependencies:** Pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and JavaScript with high performance at 60 FPS.
* **Dynamic N-Body Field Calculations:** Real-time velocity and distance calculations applied per particle.
* **Interactive Controls:** Toggle between attractors and repulsors, tweak gravity strengths, adjust spatial friction (damping), and alter particle density dynamically.
* **Presets:** Includes instant galactic orbit and explosive supernova initializations.
* **Speed-Based Chromatic Shift:** Particles shift color dynamically in HSL space as velocity changes.
* **Responsive Layout:** Works smoothly on mobile touchscreens and high-resolution desktop displays.

---

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Place Anchors:** Tap or click anywhere on the canvas to place a gravitational attractor or repulsor.
2. **Switch Tool:** Use the control sidebar to toggle between placing **Attractors** (Cyan) or **Repulsors** (Pink).
3. **Adjust Sliders:** Tweak gravity parameters, friction, and particle density in real time.
4. **Presets:** Click **Galaxy** to form orbital spirals or **Supernova** to burst particles outward.
